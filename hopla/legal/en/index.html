Privacy Policy — Hoplà Map

Last updated: November 15, 2026 — version 1.1

This policy explains, in plain words, what personal data the iOS app Hoplà Map (name shown under the icon: “Hoplà”) collects, why, how long it is kept and what your rights are. It is written in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act (“Informatique et Libertés”).

In short: your location is only shared with the people in the circles you join; we sell no data; the ad on the free plan is non-personalized and never receives your location or identity; your data is hosted in the European Union (Paris) and automatically deleted after 48 hours, 7 days or 30 days depending on your plan; you can delete your account at any time from the app.

Locations from an iPad or an Apple Watch are never collected: they are only used to view your circle. The Apple Watch receives your circle's information from your iPhone.

We do not use your location for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not pass it on to any ad network. There is no third-party analytics or behavioral tracking tool in the app.

Hoplà Map does not ask for any sensitive data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR. However, a location can reveal life habits: this is why every located person is informed, stays in control (pause, bubble, leave the circle) and can delete their account.

3. Who sees what

  • The members of your circles see your location, battery level, places and alerts, according to your sharing mode and your per-circle settings. Circle admins can manage its members.
  • A child in a circle can always see who is able to locate them. If they pause sharing (when the circle allows it), the admins are notified.
  • Nobody else: no member of another circle, no third party, no ad network.
  • Access rights are enforced by the database itself (row-level security).

4. Retention periods

Locations and related events are permanently deleted from the server (not just hidden) by an automatic job, every hour.

DataFree planHoplà +Hoplà Max
Location history, place events, trips48 hours7 days30 days

The period applied depends on the plan of the located person or, if an active member of their circle subscribes to a higher plan, on that plan (a parent's subscription benefits the whole circle). The maximum period is 30 days.

Other periods:

  • Last known location: kept as long as your account exists, and replaced at each update; it is no longer sent while you are paused.
  • Invitations: 48 hours.
  • Queued notifications: 7 days at most (24 hours of retries).
  • SOS alerts: kept with their location while the service needs them, then deleted with the corresponding history; message and replies erased with the account.
  • Notification tokens: as long as the device is registered; deleted as soon as Apple reports them invalid or when you sign out.
  • Profile photo: as long as you keep it; deleted with your account.
  • Account, profile, circles: until your account is deleted.
  • Support correspondence: 3 years at most after the last exchange.
  • After account deletion: your data is erased from the live database immediately. The host's technical backups are purged of it within 30 days at most.

7. Cookies, trackers and advertising

The mobile app does not use browser cookies. However, the Google AdMob advertising SDK may access device identifiers and technical information.

  • On the free plan only, an ad banner is shown below the map. It is non-personalized: it is not based on your profile or your history.
  • No location data, no identity and no circle content is sent to Google. Google only receives what is needed to show an ad (IP address, technical device and app information, delivery and measurement data).
  • We do not ask for Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, because we do no cross-app tracking.
  • Consent (UMP): on first launch in the countries concerned (EEA, United Kingdom), Google's consent management platform (User Messaging Platform) shows you a form. You can change your choice later in Settings → Manage ad consent. Even without consent to personalization, non-personalized ads may be served.
  • No ads for Hoplà + and Max subscribers, on child accounts and during an SOS.
  • You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Complaints: if you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France — www.cnil.fr/plaintes — or with the data protection authority of your country, without prejudice to any court action.

Providing your location is necessary for the location feature to work; without it, the app cannot fulfill its main function. No fully automated decision producing legal effects is made about you.

9. Minors

Hoplà Map is not in the App Store “Kids” category: it is meant for a general audience, including families. In France, a minor under 15 cannot consent alone to the processing of their data (the age may differ in your country): their profile must be created or approved by a parent or legal guardian, who gives this consent and can withdraw it. Between 15 and 18, the minor can consent alone, but we encourage them to talk about it with their parents.

Safeguards for child profiles: the child can always see who is able to locate them; no ads, no outgoing links and no purchases are possible from a child profile; the child is never located without knowing it. A parent who wants a child's data deleted can write to us at the contact address. If you think a child under 15 is using the app without parental consent, write to us: we will delete the account.

10. Security

  • Encrypted communications (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and the server.
  • Access control at the database level: a member can only read the data of the circles they belong to; sensitive functions and service keys are never reachable from the app.
  • Authentication delegated to Apple (we store no passwords).
  • Automatic purge of locations, data minimization, bubble-mode blurring done on the device.
  • Notification tokens and API secrets stored on the server side only.
  • Hosting in secure data centers in the EU.

No system is infallible. In the event of a data breach likely to put you at risk, we will notify the CNIL within 72 hours and, if the risk is high, the people concerned.

Reminder: the profile photo is stored in a space reachable by link; don't use an image you don't want to be shared.

Hoplà Map lets people who trust each other (friends, couples, families, roommates, travel groups) voluntarily share their location within circles, get arrival and departure alerts for places, trigger an SOS, view a trip history and, depending on the plan, get a driving report or crash detection. Hoplà Map is designed for transparent, consensual sharing. You agree to:

The located person always knows who sees them, and can stop sharing at any time.

3.2 Legal consequences

Locating a person without their consent may be a criminal offense. Under French law, for example: invasion of privacy (Art. 226-1 of the Criminal Code, up to 1 year in prison and a €45,000 fine), unlawful collection of personal data (Art. 226-18, up to 5 years and €300,000), harassment or domestic abuse through surveillance (Art. 222-33-2-1 et seq.), in addition to damages. Similar laws exist in other countries. We will cooperate with judicial authorities upon lawful request. If you are being tracked without your consent, contact the police or a local support line (in France: 3919), or write to us.

3.3 Other prohibited uses

You may not: bypass technical limits or paid plans; try to access other users' data; disrupt the service (abusive automation, reverse engineering, overload); trigger false SOS alerts; share unlawful content (for example in a profile photo or names); use the service for commercial purposes or to monitor employees.

4. Plans and subscriptions

4.1 Plans

  • Free: 1 circle, 2 places, 48 hours of history, live map, SOS, “I got here safely”, a non-personalized ad banner.

  • Hoplà Max (monthly or yearly): everything in Hoplà +, unlimited places and scheduled alerts, 30 days of history, weekly driving report.

Core safety features (SOS, “I got here safely”) stay free. In France, prices are €2.99/month or €24.99/year for Hoplà + and €4.99/month or €39.99/year for Hoplà Max, including VAT. The price shown in the App Store in your country on the date of purchase is the one that applies.

SOS and crash detection are aids, not emergency services.

  • Crash detection relies on your phone's sensors; it may miss a crash (false negative) or trigger by mistake (false positive). It does not replace Apple's built-in Crash Detection or any emergency system. No guarantee of detection, timing or delivery is given.
  • Alerts, locations and notifications depend on factors outside our control: battery, network, GPS, iOS permissions, Airplane Mode, Focus, device settings, Apple services. Locations may be inaccurate or delayed.
  • Don't rely solely on the app for your safety or anyone else's.
